A comment thread with one form. The component shows the thread and the form. The page stores what a reader sends, and the author approves or removes it.

  1. Press Reply on a comment. The form moves under it. Cancel moves it back.
  2. Write a reply and press Send. It shows as Awaiting review, only for you.
  3. In the author's desk, press Approve. The comment is published and can be answered.
  4. Press Remove. A comment with replies leaves a placeholder. One without replies goes.
