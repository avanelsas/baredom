x-drag-panel A panel the user can pick up with a pointer or the keyboard. It reports where it was dropped and never moves itself — the handler below is what actually reorders the DOM. The component needs no server and no network. Out of the box this page runs the whole board from one x-drop-zone-drop handler that calls insertBefore. The in-flight controls further down are the optional extra for apps that do have a round trip to wait on. Live Controls

Optional — the in-flight window Only relevant when the move has to be confirmed by something slow. Turn this on and the app sets pending on the card and pending + pending-index on the target zone, then clears them when the fake server answers. Nothing in the board moves until it does.
Board Keyboard: Tab to a card's handle, press Space to pick it up, ↑/↓ to cycle zones, Enter to drop, Esc to cancel.
